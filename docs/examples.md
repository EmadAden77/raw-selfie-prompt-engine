# ثلاثة أمثلة فعلية من محرك Fallback

هذه البرومبتات الثلاثة أُنشئت باستخدام نفس تركيب **Rule Builder → Creative Expander → Prompt Assembler** والبيانات وقواعد الثبات الحالية في `backend/src`، **دون أي مفتاح NVIDIA ودون استدعاء NVIDIA API**. النصوص الإنجليزية داخل الكتل هي برومبتات كاملة جاهزة للنسخ إلى ChatGPT مع رفع الصورة المرجعية هناك، والتطبيق نفسه لا يولّد صورة.

عند توليد النتيجة في مسار Backend المحلي تكون قيمة المصدر `source: "local"`. تختلف تفاصيل الخلفية باختلاف قيمة `variation`، بينما تبقى الهوية والسيارة والسيلفي الخام والمنع الصريح للجلد الشمعي والبلاستيكي ثابتة.

## المثال 1: سيلفي داخل السيارة بثوب أبيض وابتسامة خفيفة

- معرفات الاختيارات: `{"locations":"location-15","clothing":"clothing-1","expressions":"expression-2","hairstyles":"hair-1","selfieAngles":"angle-1","poses":"pose-1","carConditions":"clean-2","lighting":"light-1"}`.
- قيمة التنويع: `1`.
- النتيجة: اجتاز فحص المقدمة والإنجليزية والهوية والسيارة البيضاء والداخلية البيج والوقوف وعدم وجود معلم مشهور والسيلفي الخام وحظر المظهر الشمعي والبلاستيكي.

```text
ChatGPT, using the reference image I uploaded, generate an image of the exact same person. Preserve the exact identity from the uploaded reference image: identical face and head structure, natural asymmetry, skin tone, apparent age, eyes, brows, nose, lips, jaw, ears and body proportions. Never beautify, slim, whiten, reshape, airbrush, retouch or modify any facial feature. Do not add scars, wrinkles or artificial blemishes absent from the reference. The reference photo is the sole source for the person's identity and body; no invention or identity averaging. Keep exactly the same hairline, temple recession, visible density, strand thickness, length, curl pattern, growth directions and facial-hair boundaries as the reference. Only reposition already existing hair where its original length allows; never add volume, fill thin areas, thin out hair or invent new growth. The vehicle is always a 2022 Range Rover Sport with pearl-white exterior, sport package, black rims, black grille and LED headlights. Its interior is fixed: beige leather upholstery, beige seats, beige dashboard, beige steering wheel, beige door panels and a panoramic roof. Never change the vehicle model, exterior paint, interior color or fixed details. The vehicle is stationary, parked and not being driven in every scene. Raw real-life selfie taken by the subject with the front-facing phone camera, at a physically reachable arm distance, unless a selected mirror/reflection composition requires a visible phone reflection. Use subtle wide-angle distortion, slightly imperfect handheld framing, limited dynamic range, soft edge variation and ordinary smartphone processing. No floating camera, professional lens appearance, photographer viewpoint, studio setup or impossible arm/phone geometry. Preserve actual skin pores, subtle natural texture, appropriate perspiration where justified by weather, authentic cloth creases and naturally muted real-world colors. No wax skin, plastic skin, porcelain skin, CGI, 3D render, doll face, plastic clothing, toy-like car, airbrushed surface, beauty filter, fake cinematic lighting or exaggerated HDR. Apply the same plausible phone-camera exposure, grain, sharpness and compression to the entire scene, including face and background. Respect human anatomy, gravity, contact points, seat and body support, natural weight distribution and a realistic selfie arm pose. Every highlight, shadow and reflection must follow an identifiable scene light source; night exposures must not invent hidden lights. Clothing folds follow gravity and pose. The car doors, mirrors, steering wheel and phone must all be mechanically plausible. Resolve any conflict between a selected pose, selfie angle and light source by preserving safety, anatomy, actual camera geometry and fixed identity. Use an everyday generic Saudi setting without a named landmark, famous building, named mall, signature tower or branded destination. The car must always remain safely parked, engine off or in park; nearby background vehicles may move but never the subject car. The background must be inhabited, nonempty and context-appropriate, with natural pedestrians, distant traffic, dust or reflections as realistic. Setting: outside an ordinary villa in an unnamed everyday Saudi area, with the subject vehicle safely parked Body pose: relaxed in driver seat with free hand on steering wheel Selfie angle: handheld from the driver seat Clothing: white cotton thobe, with gravity-consistent wrinkles and fabric weight Expression: slight natural smile without altering facial identity Hair direction: naturally messy forward strands only within the reference hair's existing characteristics Vehicle surface condition: light road dust on bodywork Lighting: morning sunlight with physically consistent exposure and shadows Lively believable background detail: a passing delivery motorcycle at a plausible distance in the background. Interior detail, only where visible: subtle fingerprints on the console screen and a coffee cup in the holder. Slightly imperfect spontaneous composition and everyday realistic scene variation; do not change the subject, fixed vehicle or selections. Vertical portrait composition, 9:16 aspect ratio.
```

## ملاحظة حدود الاختبار

الاختبارات هنا تتحقق من **نصوص Fallback الناتجة وقواعدها**؛ لا تُثبت قدرة أي مولّد صور على إنتاج تطابق بصري مطلق. لم يُستخدم مفتاح NVIDIA حقيقي، ولم تُولَّد أي صورة. تتطلب خدمة NVIDIA مفتاحًا في `backend/.env` محليًا فقط، ولا يُرفع إلى GitHub. تعمل الواجهة بالعربية RTL، والبرومبت وحده بالإنجليزية.
